What your help buys.

The same things it bought in 2001: a glove that fits, a clinic a family can't afford, a bus ticket to a tryout. What's new is that you can see where it went.

Gear

Gloves and bats

Equipment drives at home and to Panama and Cuba.

Instruction from people who played the game at a high level.

A shot at the next level

Travel to tryouts and showcases, and calls to college coaches.

How it's recorded.

Gifts go into the Family Fund and come out as grants to vendors, by purpose, on a public ledger. The fund is being set up: its ledger shows no entries yet, and we'll say so until it does.
