Athletes · NIL

Why we built NIL33.

Since July 2025, college athletes can be paid by their schools as well as by brands. That's a lot of new money and a lot of new promises. We've watched players get squeezed for twenty-five years. NIL33 is how our players, coaches and creators take deals without the games.

The rules

Four rules. No exceptions.

These are the same rules on every deal, printed before anyone signs.

  • Flat fee or unpaidA set fee for a set term, or unpaid and disclosed.
  • No percentage, everNo commissions, no revenue share, no cut of a player's money.
  • Signed, then publicEvery countersigned agreement goes on a ledger anyone can read.
  • A parent's signatureUnder 18, nothing happens without one.

Our generation knows what we went through. Now it's our turn to look out for the ones coming up.
