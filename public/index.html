2003 · Tucson

The pro camp before a record game

We ran the two-day pro camp for the 24 Hours for Africa women's game at Tucson Electric Park, and our coaches worked the game through the night. SABR lists its 127–110 final as the record for a planned marathon game.

NIL33 · college and pro

Fair deals, in the open

A flat fee for a set term, in writing, then on a public ledger. No percentage of a player's money, ever.

 Under 18 · protected

Young athletes first

A young player's record belongs to the family. Nothing about a player under 18 is public, and nothing happens without a parent's signature.

 Records · on chain

Check, don't trust

Our history archive is fingerprinted and anchored on a public blockchain, so nobody can quietly change it. Records are history, never a share of anything.

Who runs this today

Live the Dream Athletics was founded in 2001 by Kevan Burns. Today it and NIL33 are programs of UnyKorn LLC, a Wyoming company that builds the records and tools behind them.
