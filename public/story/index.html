Our story

Then, and now.

The old era was gloves, clinics, camps and phone calls to college coaches. The new era keeps all of that and adds two things the game never had: fair, open deals for athletes, and a record nobody can quietly change. Every line here links to its source.

The founder, as a player

  1. 1995
