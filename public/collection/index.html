How a record works

Four steps. Check any of them.

  1. Photograph and storyThe piece is photographed and its story written: what it is, when it's from, who is in it, where the original is kept.
  2. FingerprintThe record is hashed with SHA-256. Change one letter or one pixel and the fingerprint changes.
  3. BundleAll the fingerprints combine into one root (a Merkle tree), so one number stands for the whole set.
  4. AnchorThe root is written to Base, a public blockchain. Anyone can rebuild it from the files and match it, without asking us.
