The people who shaped it.

Coaches, mentors and players from twenty-five years in the game. Each card says how we know them, in our own words, next to public facts with their sources.

In memory · Coaches who shaped me

John Mayotte

Kevan's college coach at Troy. Years later, when Kevan was director of baseball at Montverde Academy, he hired Coach Mayotte as head coach for the season; he and his wife lived on campus.

Troy head coach 1991-2002; led Troy to the 1993 NCAA Division II College World Series; 386 wins at Troy. Died January 2022.

Cam Walker

Kevan's college coach at Indian Hills Community College. He came to Montverde Academy for Scout Day, so both of Kevan's college coaches helped run the camp.

Indian Hills head coach 1993-2018; NJCAA Baseball Coaches Association Hall of Fame (2012).

Rick Schu

Kevan's hitting coach in the Arizona Diamondbacks system; 'the best hitting coach I ever had.' Kevan visited him at Nationals spring training (and brought a case of Budweiser).

Coached with four Diamondbacks affiliates 1998-2001, then Arizona's minor league hitting coordinator and later its major league hitting coach; Washington Nationals hitting coach 2013-2017.

Came to Kevan's grandmother's house and signed him for the Arizona Diamondbacks
