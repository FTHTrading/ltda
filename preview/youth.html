Young athletes · under 18

Protected until they're ready.

Showcases, rankings and recruiting pages can put a kid's height, school, hometown and numbers in front of anyone with a browser. Exposure is good. Exposure to everyone isn't. We're building a record the family controls, so a young player's information reaches the right people and nobody else.

In design: no player data collected yet
How it works

Six promises. Built in, not bolted on.

  1. A parent opens itUnder 18, nothing exists without a parent's signature. At 18, the record passes to the player.
  2. Nothing is publicNo public profile for any player under 18. Not a name, not a school, not a hometown.
  3. Facts are signedThe event that timed the sixty signs the time. The coach signs the milestone. Nobody can pad a number.
  4. Answers, not filesA recruiter asks "Is he 16 or older?" or "Under 7.0 in the sixty?" and gets a signed yes or no. Never the birth date, the transcript or the address.
  5. The parent approves who asksEach coach or program gets access for a set time and a set list of questions. The family can take it back at any time.
  6. Every look is loggedThe family sees who asked what, and when. Nobody looks without leaving a mark.

The fine print
  • This is in design. No player's information is collected until the parent-consent process has been reviewed by counsel for children's privacy and school-records law.
  • Player information stays encrypted and off any blockchain. Only a fingerprint of a signed fact is ever anchored, and it reveals nothing about the player.
  • We never sell or license a young player's information. Not to brands, not to scouts, not to anyone.
