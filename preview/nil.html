NIL · name, image, likeness

Paid in the open.

College athletes can be paid for their name, image and likeness, and since July 2025 schools can share revenue with them directly. That's a lot of new money and a lot of new promises. NIL33 is how our players, coaches and creators do it without the games.

Why it matters

Our generation knows what we went through: the handshake deals, the promises nobody wrote down. Now it's our turn to look out for the ones coming up, the way the people before us looked out for us. That's why NIL, and every contract, gets done in the open.

Kevan Burns · founder
The rules

Five rules. No exceptions.

Live

Flat fee or unpaid

Every deal is a flat fee for a set term, or unpaid and disclosed. Nothing tied to how much a player earns.

Live

No percentage, ever

No commissions. No revenue share. No cut of a player's money, and nothing tied to recovered funds.

Live

Signed, then public

An agreement goes on the public ledger once it's countersigned. Anyone can read the ledger, as a page or as data.

Live

A parent's signature

Under 18, nothing happens without a parent's signature. How we protect young athletes

How it ties together

From a kid's first glove to a signed deal.

  • The record we build with families when a player is young carries into college and pro.
  • Endorsement income stays with the athlete. Nothing is ever borrowed against it.
  • The deal and the history sit side by side, both where the player can always find them.
