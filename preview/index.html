RWA · real-world records

The collection

Bats, jerseys, scrapbooks and photos from the game, each with a record: its story, where the original is kept, and a fingerprint written to a public blockchain so nobody can quietly change it. A record is history, never a share of anything.

33 records anchored 6 pieces being finished NIL · name, image, likeness

Fair deals for players

For college and pro players: endorsement deals at a flat fee, in writing, on a public ledger. No percentage of a player's money. Under 18, nothing happens without a parent's signature.

Ledger live ID check in build Under 18 · protected

Young athletes

A young player's record belongs to the family. Nothing about a player under 18 is public. A recruiter gets the yes or no they asked for, not the file, and only with a parent's okay.

In design
NIL33

Paid in the open.

Since July 2025, college athletes can be paid by their schools as well as by brands. That's a lot of new money and a lot of new promises. NIL33 is how our players, coaches and creators take deals without the games.

How NIL33 worksSee the ledger
  • Flat fee or unpaidA set fee for a set term, or unpaid and disclosed.
  • No percentage, everNo commissions, no revenue share, no cut of a player's money.
  • Signed, then publicEvery countersigned agreement goes on a ledger anyone can read.
  • A parent's signatureUnder 18, nothing happens without one.
